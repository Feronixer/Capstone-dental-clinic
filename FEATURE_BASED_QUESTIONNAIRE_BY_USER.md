- **1 = Strongly Disagree**

## Notes

- All features listed are based on actual system capabilities verified from the codebase and test cases
- Some features may have different access levels between Staff and Dentist (Admin) roles
- Notification features work across all roles but with role-specific content
- Patient record access is available to Staff and Dentist but with different permission levels
- Email notifications are sent automatically by the system when certain actions occur
- The chatbot FAQ and settings are managed by Dentist and Staff but accessible to all patients
- Activity logs are only accessible to Dentist (Admin) role for system auditing
