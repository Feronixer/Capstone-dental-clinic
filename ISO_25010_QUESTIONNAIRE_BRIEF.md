## 1. Functional Suitability

### a. Functional Completeness
**The system has all the functions I need.**

**+ Dentist (Admin):** "I can manage all clinic operations including creating staff accounts, viewing dashboard statistics (patients, appointments, staff counts), managing appointments, configuring announcements and services, viewing activity logs, and accessing all patient records."

**+ Staff:** "I can schedule and manage appointments, create patient records with medical history, search for patients, send records to patients, handle appointment requests, and view daily appointment statistics."
