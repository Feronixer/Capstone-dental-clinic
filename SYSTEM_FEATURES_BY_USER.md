# System Features by User Role

## Dentist / Admin Perspective
- I can review new appointment requests and approve, reassign, or decline them.
- I can manage the appointment calendar, including blocking time slots and viewing schedules by provider.
- I can access complete patient records, including treatment history, charts, and uploaded files.
- I can update patient data, add clinical notes, and record post-procedural information.
- I can send announcements, service updates, and policy changes through the content management tools.
- I can configure services, pricing, treatment packages, and mail templates that appear on the portals.
- I can manage staff accounts, assign roles, reset credentials, and track staff activity.
- I can inspect the activity log to audit every action taken across the system.
- I can monitor notification queues, resend messages, and track delivery status.
- I can manage chatbot FAQs, knowledge-base entries, and other self-help content.
- I can view analytics, reports, and dashboards that highlight clinic performance.
- I can customize system settings, environment configurations, and integration options.
- I can manage backups, review migration status, and supervise system maintenance tasks.

## Staff Perspective
- I can log in to the staff portal with secure credentials and personalized access.
- I can view my daily schedule, upcoming appointments, and real-time status updates.
- I can create, edit, or cancel appointments on behalf of patients when needed.
- I can confirm arrivals, mark completions, and document procedure outcomes.
- I can register new patients, collect demographics, and maintain contact information.
- I can update patient histories, dental records, allergies, and treatment plans.
- I can upload, organize, and access patient documents, x-rays, and consent forms.
- I can send reminders, follow-up messages, and notifications to patients.
- I can coordinate with dentists by leaving notes, tagging users, and routing tasks.
- I can mark notifications as read, filter by status, and respond to urgent alerts.
- I can use built-in search and filtering to quickly find patients, services, or logs.
- I can access guides, FAQs, and step-by-step documentation directly in the portal.

## Patient Perspective
- I can create an account, verify my email, and sign in to my personalized portal.
- I can request new appointments, choose preferred dates, and set treatment reasons.
- I can view appointment status updates, approval notices, and rescheduling messages.
- I can review my past visits, treatment notes, and dentist recommendations.
- I can update my profile details, contact information, and account password.
- I can upload required documents securely for clinic review before visits.
- I can receive notifications for reminders, approvals, and follow-up care.
- I can read announcements, service updates, and promotional offers from the clinic.
- I can consult the chatbot FAQ and help guides to answer common questions.
- I can submit feedback, respond to forms, and communicate with clinic staff securely.
