# Feature Capabilities by User Role

Every portal voice below is written in first person so each user can see exactly what they can do in the system.

## Dentist (Admin Portal)
- I log in to the admin portal with my credentials and change my password whenever needed.
- I update my own profile details so my contact information stays current.
- I open my dashboard to see real-time totals for patients, appointments, staff members, and today’s schedule.
- I create, edit, and remove user accounts for admins, staff, and patients while assigning the right roles.
- I register new users with complete personal details, including automatic age calculation from their birthdays.
- I reset or change passwords for any user who needs help signing in.
- I manage every appointment by creating, editing, rescheduling, canceling, and updating appointment statuses.
- I review, approve, or deny walk-in and reschedule requests that patients submit.
- I study appointment statistics to understand trends and make scheduling decisions.
- I block out clinic time slots so no one can book during unavailable hours.
- I publish, update, archive, or delete announcements that the clinic needs to share.
- I add, edit, and organize the list of dental services with prices, durations, and descriptions.
- I build and maintain email templates for confirmations, reminders, cancellations, reschedules, and follow-ups.
- I craft chatbot FAQ entries so ToothTalk responds with up-to-date answers.
- I adjust chatbot welcome messages and behaviors to guide patients better.
- I update the ticker text on the public site and enable or disable it as needed.
- I search and view any patient’s full medical and dental records whenever I need details.
- I audit every action in the system through detailed activity logs with filters for user, module, action, and date.
- I rely on my dashboard metrics to guide clinic performance and operational planning.
- I configure global system settings and user role permissions from the admin tools.
- I read, filter, and clear notifications about appointments, user activity, and system events.
- I turn appointment and activity data into reports that support clinic decisions.

## Staff Portal
- I sign in through the staff portal with my username and change my password when necessary.
- I keep my profile information updated so colleagues can reach me easily.
- I open my dashboard to see today’s appointments, key statistics, and recent patient activity.
- I set up, edit, reschedule, cancel, and complete appointments for patients under my care.
- I review patient appointment requests and decide whether to approve or deny them.
- I rely on the system to block scheduling conflicts and respect the dentist’s blocked time.
- I search for patients by name, username, email, or ID to pull up their records fast.
- I create, update, and clean up patient records with detailed treatment information.
- I add or revise patient medical and dental history entries so charts stay accurate.
- I write, edit, and remove progress notes that track treatment status and next steps.
- I open a patient’s full history whenever I need to review prior treatments.
- I send finalized records to patients and track which documents have already been shared.
- I monitor my notifications about appointments and clinic updates, marking them as read when done.
- I set blocked time periods when the clinic or a specific dentist is unavailable.
- I view and, when permitted, update service information to keep offerings accurate.
- I read announcements so I never miss clinic-wide news or reminders.
- I check the activity log (when granted access) to review my actions for accountability.

## Patient Portal
- I sign in with my email or username and change my password to keep my account secure.
- I edit my contact and profile details so the clinic always has my latest information.
- I open my dashboard to see appointment summaries, notifications, and shortcuts to important pages.
- I view my appointments on a calendar that shows the date, time, and visit details.
- I request a walk-in appointment by choosing a preferred time and explaining my reason.
- I submit a reschedule request when I need to move an existing appointment.
- I review my full appointment history, including past visits and upcoming bookings.
- I read notifications, filter them by status, mark them as read, and remove the ones I no longer need.
- I glance at the notification bell to see new alerts and open recent updates instantly.
- I rely on the system to notify me about confirmations, cancellations, reschedules, and record updates.
- I get email reminders and confirmations for every appointment-related event.
- I chat with ToothTalk to ask about clinic hours, services, prices, and appointment steps.
- I pick quick chatbot options to jump straight to the answers I need most often.
- I read my post-procedure records, medical history, and dental history that staff share with me.
- I browse clinic announcements so I stay updated on schedules, promos, and important news.
- I review the list of dental services, including descriptions, prices, and how long each procedure takes.
- I check the About Us page to learn more about the clinic and where it is located.
